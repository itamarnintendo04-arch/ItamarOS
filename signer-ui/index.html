Sign IPA in your browser

A real local code-signing tool powered by an open-source zsign WebAssembly engine. Files are processed in this browser, not uploaded to ItamarOS servers.

Important: Signing requires a valid Apple-issued signing certificate .p12 and a matching provisioning profile .mobileprovision. A free Apple ID alone is not sufficient to provide these files here. This does not install apps automatically or bypass iOS security checks.

Privacy and safety

      
IPA, certificate, private key, provisioning profile and password stay in browser memory during signing. Nothing is sent to an ItamarOS signing backend. Certificate data is not saved to localStorage, IndexedDB or your ItamarOS account list. Closing the page clears the active session.

      
Signing an IPA does not make it safe, prove that its source is trustworthy, or guarantee it will install on your iPhone. Do not ignore Apple security alerts. Apple may reject incompatible signatures, entitlements and devices.
