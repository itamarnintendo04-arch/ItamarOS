Device mirroring protocols

Apple AirPlay

On a supported iPhone, iPad or Mac, use the system's Screen Mirroring menu and choose an AirPlay-compatible TV or receiver. Standard third-party webpages do not become AirPlay receivers just by having an AirPlay button.

Android / Google Cast

On supported Android devices, use the system Cast / Screen Cast controls and choose your Google Cast-compatible display. A full native Cast receiver would require a registered receiver application and compatible device.

4K at 60 FPS

ItamarMirror asks for up to 3840×2160 at 60 FPS when the browser permits it. The actual resolution and frame rate depend on the source screen, device, encoder, connection and receiver. No 4K60 guarantee is made.
